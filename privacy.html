Privacy policy - My Personal Tracker

Developer: My Personal Tracker. Effective date: 26 January 2027.

Contact for privacy questions, access or deletion: mypersonaltracker.app@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Your records (expenses, health records, medicines, to-dos, steps, office hours, habits and habit place reminders, Shopkeeper customer records, documents, notes, saved passwords, debit and credit cards and the rest) are kept on your phone. Your PIN and fingerprint lock never leave it. Saved cards are kept encrypted on the phone only, are never uploaded or put in a backup, and no CVV or PIN ever leaves the phone.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number and email (checked with a one-time code), app version, phone model, language and this install's id, kept in the app owner's Google Sheet so the owner can run your account and contact you about the app.
  • Backups (saved only where you choose): the app saves backups only in a folder you pick on your phone, or in a folder in your own Google Drive. Each backup file is locked on your phone with a key tied to your verified email, and the app does not send it to the app owner. A backup can hold everything the app keeps: health records, money records, files and documents, office location and attendance, habit places, screen time, Shopkeeper customers, loans and occasions (names and phone numbers), notes and photos. The owner's script holds the key (it is handed to your phone after the email check), so the owner could technically open an older cloud backup, but does not.
  • Older cloud backups: earlier versions of the app saved a locked daily backup in the app owner's Google Drive, in a folder of your own. The app no longer makes these. The ones already saved stay there so you can still restore them (the owner's script can only list and return them to you); they are deleted with your account.
  • Prices: to show what your investments are worth, the stock and fund codes you track (not your amounts or name) are sent to Yahoo Finance and mfapi.in, which answer with the latest prices.
  • Gold, silver and exchange rates: the Gold & Silver tool asks public rate websites (Yahoo Finance, gold-api.com and the ECB rate through frankfurter.app) for the world gold and silver price and the dollar-to-rupee rate. These requests carry no personal data and not your holdings.
  • Problem reports you choose to send, with a user id and the phone details shown on the form.
  • Error lines (diagnostics): once a day, the error lines the app recorded on your phone (Settings > Recent problems), with emails, numbers and file names taken out, are sent with your user id and app version so problems can be found and fixed. They are kept for 90 days. Switch this off in Settings > Recent problems.

Phone permissions

Location (office punch-in and habit place reminders, also when the app is closed), activity (steps), usage access (screen time), notifications (reminders, alarms, timers and a Health check-in every 2 hours with your steps and water) and camera (scanning documents and photographing receipts and reports). Each is asked for only when you use the feature, and what they read stays on your phone unless named above. The app does not read your SMS (a bank message you paste into it yourself is read on the phone and kept only as the entry you save), your contacts or record audio.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared with advertisers. It is stored with Google (Sheets, Drive) to run the features above, prices are fetched from Yahoo Finance and mfapi.in, and gold, silver and exchange rates from Yahoo Finance, gold-api.com and frankfurter.app, with no personal data. Reader files you save as Excel, Word, PDF and the like are made on your phone and go only where you choose in Android's save or share sheet.

Earlier versions

Earlier versions of the app had a Messages feature (chats and calls). It has been removed: the app no longer sends, receives or stores messages, reads contacts or makes calls, and its chats are no longer kept in backups. Reports and safety copies made with it are deleted 365 days after they were made, and the app owner deletes the chat data it kept in Google Firebase. Earlier versions could also read bank SMS on the phone to add expenses and bills; that has been removed too, and the app no longer asks for SMS access.

Retention and deletion

Backup files you save in your own folders stay with you; the app owner does not keep them. Older cloud backups (see above) are kept until you delete your account. Use Settings > Delete my account in the app, or see how to delete your account.
