Delete your My Personal Tracker account

In the app

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Without the app

Email mypersonaltracker.app@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete my account". It is done within 7 days and you get a reply when it is.

What is deleted

  • Your account details (name, mobile number, email).
  • Your old cloud backups, if any (moved to the bin and removed for good by Google within 30 days).
  • Your tool settings.
  • The error lines the app sent from your phone.

What is kept

  • Problem reports, which carry a user id but no name or address.
  • From the removed Messages feature: reports and safety copies, kept by the app owner until they are 365 days old and then deleted.

The data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
